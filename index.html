How we operate

BB Systems uses agent-supported workflows for planning, delegated execution, review, memory, and reporting. Product state is reported from evidence: a local build, test run, or handoff is not treated as proof of production deployment.

Material decisions and delivery work are recorded with sources and verification evidence so claims can be traced to what was actually observed.
